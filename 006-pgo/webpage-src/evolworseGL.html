HEAD Pokémon GO Great League evolution downgrades BODYSTART

The following forms can see the product of their statistics reduced when they evolve. All 4,096 IV configurations are considered for the given League. A "partial" relationship indicates that some configurations are reduced on evolution. A "pure" relationship indicates that all configurations are reduced on evolution. An "absolute" relationship indicates that the best configuration of the evolved form has a lower stat product than the worst configuration of the base form.
