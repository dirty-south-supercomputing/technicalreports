Site calculations

Anyone claiming a list of optimum counters or even the best IVs for a form is making some assumptions. There are very few universally optimal orderings in Pokémon GO. Even the best IVs for a given Pokémon in a given league involve some opinion—usually that higher base product of EffA, EffD, and MHP implies a strictly superior IV set. When you hear “#1 IV” outside of Master League, that means “#1 base stat product”. Why? Because base stat product is a single number, easily calculated and easily compared, and not an obviously bad statistic. Regardless, I provide each League sorted by the geometric mean (cube root of the base stat product), arithmetic mean (base stats average), bulk (MHP * EffD), MHP, EffA, and EffD. Note that the Shadow variant of a form having the same level and IVs has exactly the same base stat product. Unless you believe every Shadow form equivalent to its regular form, this ought be sufficient to demonstrate that base stat product is an incomplete measure.

Determining optimal counters is a more complex task, and any such list will represent the opinions and assumptions of the listmaker. Herein I describe exactly how I determine my selections, justify my choices, and describe where I know they fall short.

Max attackers

Probably the most straightforward determinations. We assume that our Max Attackers are only operating in the Max phase of battle, meaning they're never attacked, and only use their Max Attacks. This reduces the calculation to maximizing power, where power is a function of EffA, level, Max Level, whether the attack has Max or G-Max stats, and type effectiveness of the attack. This is pretty uncontestable. If we considered the main phase of battle, things would get significantly more complex: we'd basically be solving the Max tanks question that follows, and adding the result to the result already calculated here. But that doesn't consider your other two tanks, which would be pretty foolish. Considering those two tanks is equivalent to solving the Max teams problem.
