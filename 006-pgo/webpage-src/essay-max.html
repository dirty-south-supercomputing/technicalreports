What's important for Max Battles?

tl;dr: evolve your shit. get type advantage. (and check out this table)

For a given Max Battle opponent, we can affect the following variables:

  • Pokémon species and form
  • Individual Pokémon properties (IVs and level)
  • Fast attack (fast attack implies Max Attack)
  • Charged attack (even if we have two, we'll probably only use one, if any)
  • Adventure Effect in use (Dynamax Cannon, Behemoth Blade, Behemoth Bash)
  • Whether a Max Mushroom is in use

What's most important for successfully winning a Max Battle? Our team of three must inflict damage quickly enough that the opponent is knocked out before we are. A quick refresher on mechanics: A Max Battle cycles through two phases: the main phase and the Max phase. During the main phase, the opponent uses charged attacks targeting one or all active Pokémon. Active Pokémon attack with fast and charged attacks, doing damage and building up the Max Meter. When the Max Meter is full, the battle switches to Max phase, during which the opponent does not attack, and each active Pokémon uses three Max Moves. Pokémon can be freely substituted during the main phase, and substituted once during the transition to Max phase. Besides Dynamax and Gigantamax Pokémon, only Crowned Sword Zacian, Crowned Shield Zamazenta, and Eternatus can participate in Max Battles. Gigantamax Pokémon have a fixed G-Max attack unique to that species. Dynamax Pokémon have a Max attack based on the type of their fast attack.

A sensible strategy is to employ two “tanks”—Pokémon resistant to (some of) the opponent's possible attacks, having high defense and/or stamina and fast move of as few turns as possible. These will absorb blows from the opponent, charge the Max Meter, and ideally never be used in the Max phase. An “attacker” will use G-Max or Max attacks to deal the majority of our damage, and ideally never be used in the main phase. It's easiest to determine the best Max Attackers for a given opponent: we can leave aside everything related to defense and stamina—the only important characteristics are EffA and the Max Attack. EffA is determined by adding the form's base attack stat to the Pokémon's IVA, and multiplying by the Combat Power Multiplier, a function of level. fully evolve your Pokémon; don't use Shuckle

The lowest base ATK capable of Max Attacks is Shuckle, with 17. The highest is Crowned Sword Zacian, with 332. Assuming IVA of 15, 347 is more than 10x the power of shameless outlier Shuckle's 32. Nothing else one can do comes close to such a difference. Gyarados with 237 (+15 = 252) has 5.72x the raw power of poor unevolved Magikarp with 29 (+15 = 44) At this time (2026-11), no Max-capable Pokémon has a higher ATK than its fully evolved form, so always ensure your Max Attackers are fully evolved, and never use Shuckle. Considering only fully evolved forms, our lowest ATK is Blissey, with 129. Crowned Sword Zacian has only 2.4x the power of Blissey, less than the 2.56x multiplier due a type effectiveness of +2. This is an extreme example, of course; a more representative one might be the Rhyhorn family. Rhydon is a 1.58x multiplier over Rhyhorn; Rhyperior is in turn about 1.09x as powerful as Rhydon (a 1.72x multiplier over Rhyhorn). All else being equal, you want Pokémon from the top half of this list.

it's type effectiveness, stupid

That's right: all else being equal, Blissey hitting for neutral effectiveness throws more damage per Max Attack than Crowned Sword Zacian's mighty Behemoth Blade at a type effectiveness of -2. A level 50 Blissey with a perfect 15 IVA having a Max Level of 3 but hitting for effectiveness of -2 throws significantly less damage than a level 20 Blissey with a mere 10 IVA having a Max Level of 1 but achieving neutral effectiveness. Two levels of type effectiveness difference correspond to a 2.56x multiplier; a single level is a 1.6x multiplier. A Level 1 Max Attack with +1 type effectiveness is equal to a Level 2 G-Max Attack with neutral effectiveness. A Level 1 Max Attack with +2 type effectiveness is stronger than a Level 3 G-Max Attack with Dynamax Cannon in effect but with neutral effectiveness. A single level of type effectiveness is 3x the boost of STAB.

level, max level, and g-max

Level 50 is about a 1.41x multiplier over Level 20. Level 40 is about a 1.32x multiplier over same, and level 30 about 1.22x. Similar boosts are available by raising your attack's Max Level. A Max Attack at Max Level 3 is exactly 1.4x as strong as Level 1, almost the same difference as promoting from level 20 to level 50 (at a significant savings in Stardust, Candy, and Candy XL). A G-Max Attack at Max Level 3 is about 1.29x as strong as Level 1, similar to the difference between level 20 and level 40, while costing no Stardust and less Candy (though some Candy XL). Max Level 2 to 3 is about a 1.17x multiplier; 1 to 2 is exactly 1.2x. G-Max 3 is a 1.13x multiplier over 2, which is a 1.14x multiplier over 1. If Dynamax Cannon is in effect, it's as if the Max Level increased by 1, except for Max Level 3. Max Level 3 plus Dynamax Cannon is a 1.8x multiplier over Max Level 1. For G-Max, the multiplier is about 1.57x over Max Level 1—still formidable! Remember that Dynamax Cannon does not affect Zacian Crowned Sword nor Zamazenta Crowned Shield. G-Max Level 1 is a 1.4x multiplier over regular Max Attacks at Max Level 1. The G-Max bonus decreases as the Max Level rises, but a G-Max at Max Level 3 is still 1.28x as powerful as a Max Attack at Max Level 3.

STAB and weather boost

STAB and weather boost are good for a 1.2x multiplier each. By all means, get them if you can, but certainly don't sacrifice type advantage for them. All G-Max Pokémon have STAB for their G-Max attack.

Behemoth Blade Adventure Effect

Behemoth Blade's Adventure Effect provides a flat multiplier of 1.05x for Max Battles. This is always less than the effect of Dynamax Cannon, but unlike Dynamax Cannon it applies to Zacian and Zamazenta, and also affects all attacks during the Max Battle, not just Max Attacks.

IVA

The least important consideration. For Blissey, an IVA of 15 is a 1.03x multiplier over an IVA of 10. For Crowned Sword Zacian, the multiplier for 15 vs. 10 is 1.01x. Max-capable Pokémon cannot be caught with an IVA less than 10, but this could result from a trade. In that case, the largest multiplier due IVs would be Blissey with a 1.12x multiplier over an IVA of 0. On Zacian, 15 vs. 0 is a 1.05x multiplier. If you're losing Max Battles, it's not because of your IVs.
