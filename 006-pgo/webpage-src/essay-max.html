What's important for Max Battles?

tl;dr: evolve your shit and get type advantage

For a given Max Battle opponent, we can affect the following variables:

  • Pokémon species and form
  • Individual Pokémon properties (IVs and level)
  • Fast attack (fast attack implies Max Attack)
  • Charged attack (even if we have two, we'll probably only use one, if any)

What's most important for successfully winning a Max Battle? It's easiest to determine our best Max Attackers, assuming that they never get hit, and are only using their Max Attacks (i.e. that they are only used during the Max Attack phase). We can leave aside everything related to defense and stamina—the only important characteristics are EffA and the Max Attack. EffA is determined by adding the form's base ATK stat to the ATK IV, and multiplying by the Combat Power Multiplier, a function of level. All Max Attacks have the same power, as do all G-Max Attacks:

fully evolve your Pokémon; don't use Shuckle

The lowest base ATK capable of Max Attacks is Shuckle, with 17. The highest is Crowned Sword Zacian, with 332. Assuming IVA of 15, 347 is more than 10x the power of shameless outlier Shuckle's 32. Nothing else one can do comes close to such a difference. Gyarados with 237 (+15 = 252) has 5.72x the raw power of poor unevolved Magikarp with 29 (+15 = 44) At this time (2026-11), no Max-capable Pokémon has a higher ATK than its fully evolved form, so always ensure your Max Attackers are fully evolved, and never use Shuckle. Considering only fully evolved forms, our lowest ATK is Blissey, with 129. Crowned Sword Zacian is now only a 2.4x the power of Blissey, less than the 2.56x multiplier possible for a type effectiveness of +2. This is an extreme example, of course; a more representative one might be the Rhyhorn family. Rhydon is a 1.58x multiplier over Rhyhorn; Rhyperior is in turn about 1.09x as powerful as Rhydon (a 1.72x multiplier over Rhyhorn). All else being equal, you want Pokémon from the top half of this list.

it's type effectiveness, stupid

That's right: all else being equal, Blissey hitting for neutral effectiveness throws more damage per Max Attack than Crowned Sword Zacian's mighty Behemoth Blade at a type effectiveness of -2. A level 50 Blissey with a perfect 15 IVA having a Max Level of 3 but hitting for effectiveness of -2 throws significantly less damage than a level 20 Blissey with a mere 10 IVA having a Max Level of 1 but achieving neutral effectiveness. Two levels of type effectiveness difference correspond to a 2.56x multiplier; a single level is a 1.6x multiplier. A Level 1 Max Attack with +1 type effectiveness is equal to a Level 2 G-Max Attack with neutral effectiveness. A Level 1 Max Attack with +2 type effectiveness is stronger than a Level 3 G-Max Attack with Dynamax Cannon in effect but with neutral effectiveness. A single level of type effectiveness is 3x the boost of STAB.

level, max level, and g-max

Level 50 is about a 1.41x multiplier over Level 20. Level 40 is about a 1.32x multiplier over same, and level 30 about 1.22x. Similar boosts are available by raising your attack's Max Level. A Max Attack at Max Level 3 is exactly 1.4x as strong as Level 1, almost the same difference as promoting from level 20 to level 50 (at a significant savings in Stardust, Candy, and Candy XL). A G-Max Attack at Max Level 3 is about 1.29x as strong as Level 1, similar to the difference between level 20 and level 40, while costing no Stardust and less Candy (though some Candy XL). Max Level 2 to 3 is about a 1.17x multiplier; 1 to 2 is exactly 1.2x. G-Max 3 is a 1.13x multiplier over 2, which is a 1.14x multiplier over 1. If Dynamax Cannon is in effect, it's as if the Max Level increased by 1, except for Max Level 3. Max Level 3 plus Dynamax Cannon is a 1.8x multiplier over Max Level 1. For G-Max, the multiplier is about 1.57x over Max Level 1—still formidable! Remember that Dynamax Cannon does not affect Zacian Crowned Sword nor Zamazenta Crowned Shield. G-Max Level 1 is a 1.4x multiplier over regular Max Attacks at Max Level 1. The G-Max bonus decreases as the Max Level rises, but a G-Max at Max Level 3 is still 1.28x as powerful as a Max Attack at Max Level 3.

STAB and weather boost

STAB and weather boost are good for a 1.2x multiplier each. By all means, get them if you can, but certainly don't sacrifice type advantage for them. All G-Max Pokémon have STAB for their G-Max attack.

IVA

The least important consideration. For Blissey, an IVA of 15 is a 1.03x multiplier over an IVA of 10. For Crowned Sword Zacian, the multiplier for 15 vs. 10 is 1.01x. Max-capable Pokémon cannot be caught with an IVA less than 10, but this could result from a trade. In that case, the largest multiplier due IVs would be Blissey with a 1.12x multiplier over an IVA of 0.

On Zacian, 15 vs. 0 is a 1.05x multiplier. If you're losing Max Battles, it's not because of your IVs.
