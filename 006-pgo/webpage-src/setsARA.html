Each attack type has an effectiveness against each opponent typing ranging from -3 to +2, inclusive. It is assumed that choice of attack is made purely based on effectiveness. ARA is the average type multiplier for the selected attack type, assuming a uniform distribution of opponent typings. Higher is better. Only charged attacks are considered for purposes of determining populations. Only certain Mega Pokémon can have three charged attacks.

The maximum theoretical ARA is 324.48 / 171 ≅ 1.898. Such a Pokémon would need at least ten charged attacks, all having different types. The maximum for the usual two charged attacks is 1.468, requiring an Ice and Rock attack; several Pokémon can learn attacks of both types. The minimum is 0.86, achieved by having only Normal charged attacks.

There are 18 different attacking types, 153 distinct sets of two attacking types, and 816 distinct sets of three attacking types.
