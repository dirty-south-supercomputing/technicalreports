Each attack type has an effectiveness against each opponent typing ranging from -3 to +2, inclusive. It is assumed that choice of attack is made purely based on effectiveness. ARA is the average type multiplier for the selected attack type, assuming a uniform distribution of opponent typings. Higher is better. Only charged attacks are considered for purposes of determining populations. Only certain Mega Pokémon can have three charged attacks.
