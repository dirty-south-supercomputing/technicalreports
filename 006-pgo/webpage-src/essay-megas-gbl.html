Megas in GBL

Scopely made major changes to the Mega system in 2026:
  • Super Mega raids and Mega level 4 for selected mons
  • “Plus” attacks for selected mons, available as third charged attacks in PvP
  • Expanded Mega presence in the GBL schedule (Season 28)
  • Megas exceeding CP bounds are reduced to the maximum level satisfying the bound
These changes are likely to have more impact as Plus attacks are distributed to more Megas (it is expected that all will eventually get one), and Trainers farm Mega mons with effective configurations for GBL.

tl;dr: In ML+Mega, you need a Mega, but probably already have one. In {GL/UL}+Mega, you don't need one now, but as more Plus moves are released, I predict Megas will occupy some of the meta due primarily to greater coverage.

Are Megas important in GBL?

In Master League, absolutely; a team without a Mega can almost always be improved by adding one. This was true even before "Plus" attacks. A Mega or Primal evolution *always* enjoys a higher stat product than its base form when IVs and level are held equal. In the absence of a CP bound, it's a nobrainer to Mega up when allowed. ML wants hundos at level 50, no matter the mon, and this holds for Megas. If you've got an ideal Dragonite for Master League, you've got an ideal Mega Dragonite.

You can see here that Megas/Primals enjoy the 13 highest stat products in the Master League. The only possible downside is the type change, when applicable.

In the Ultra and Great Leagues, things are different from a stat product perspective:
  • https://nick-black.com/pgo/gmeanGL.html
  • https://nick-black.com/pgo/gmeanUL.html
We can see that *most* Megas are partial or absolute stat product downgrades under CP bounds:
  • https://nick-black.com/pgo/evolworseGL.html
  • https://nick-black.com/pgo/evolworseUL.html
A downgrade in this context means that for the same IV, the base product is lower for the evolution (which will likely be at a lower level). This is to be expected, since the Attack²/Bulk ratio generally increases across Mega Evolution. So we'd only want to use Megas here generally either for a different defensive typing or different attacks.

Plus attacks

Plus attacks aren't necessarily standouts among a mon's other charged attacks at their base level. As the mon increases its Mega Level, Plus attacks grow stronger; it is believed that Levels 2, 3 and 4 correspond to power multipliers of 1.1, 1.2, and 1.3 respectively. Mega Level 4 is worth 150% of STAB to a Plus attack, or 50% of effective damage. That's a considerable bonus, but perhaps more impactful is the change made to typing coverage with three charged attacks. I've prepared a table of all currently realizable attack type sets.

With two charged attacks, no matter their types, there are always some typings that resist both attacks (“wall you in”). This is indicated by the E-3, E-2, and E-1 columns. With three, that is no longer true. Already several configurations can hit every Pokémon in the game for at least neutral damage (hitting every Pokémon for effective damage requires at least ten different types).

So using Mega Malamar in Great League might generally be a downgrade from Little Poof Bite Size Malamar from a stats perspective, but considering typing, Mega Malamar with Dark+Fighting+PsychicPlus hits fourteen typings for neutral that Malamar with Dark+Fighting gets walled by. It does better across the board (a superset of types can only be equal or better). Furthermore, the opponents it's hitting with that surprise third attack are getting walloped by a 1.3 multiplier.

So do I need collect Megas for GL/UL?

Remember, our ML fleet works just fine with Mega Evolution. Existing mons powered up to their GL/UL bounds, however, won't fly--their Mega Evolution takes them over the CP bound (Scopely is supposedly going to introduce a mechanism to work around this, but they haven't yet, and there are no details). The *types* of IVs you want for Megas are the same as you do for bases (a-b-c where a is small, b and c are large), the exact rankings don't hold: Mega Venusaur wants 1-15-15 for GL. Venusaur wants 0-14-11. If you catch a 0-15-15 Venusaur, it's #148 (98.5%) as Venusaur and #4 (99.9%) as Mega Venusaur, both respectable. Due to the lower stats of Mega Venusaur, the Venusaur #128 actually beats the Mega Venusaur #4 statwise (122.035 vs 121.302). So which is more important, Venusaur or Mega Venusaur?

Right now Mega Venusaur has no Plus attack, so it doesn't reap the potential benefits—but it should in the future. You can only have one Mega at a time, and there's the cooldown period, and when not evolved the GL Mega will have less capability than a GL non-Mega. It can't be used for GBL outside of (recently more frequent) Mega Cups. But when evolved, it potentially gains some pretty great coverage and a formidable attack.

Had I a Mega Malamar suitable for GL+Mega, I'd certainly be running that over the Malamar I currently run in GL+Mega.
