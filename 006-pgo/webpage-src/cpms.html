Each Pokémon level maps to a fixed Combat Power Multiplier (CPM). The CPM grows less quickly as the level increases. There are five regimes of growth. Normally, Pokémon can only be powered up to Level 50, totaling 99 half-levels. An active Best Buddy is boosted by 2 half-levels, as is an active Mega Evolution having Mega Level 4. Outside of special events, the maximum level reachable is thus 52. All CPMs can be represented without error in IEEE754 64-bit floats.
